Each set: _diff.jpg (albedo, sRGB) + _nor.jpg (OpenGL normal, linear)
        + _arm.jpg (packed AO / Roughness / Metalness, linear), 1K JPG.

_arm is the glTF-convention ORM packing: AO in .r, roughness in .g, metalness
in .b. three.js reads roughness straight off .g via `roughnessMap`, which gives
real per-texel roughness instead of one flat constant per surface.
